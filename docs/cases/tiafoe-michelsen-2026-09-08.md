## Verified event

- 8 September 2026, US Open men's quarterfinal, Arthur Ashe Stadium. Alex Michelsen, 22, played his first Grand Slam quarterfinal against his friend Frances Tiafoe.
- Michelsen won the first two sets and served for the match at 5–4 in the third. Tiafoe won 5–7, 3–6, 7–5, 6–3, 7–6(6) after four hours and 38 minutes.
- Tiafoe cut his celebration short and ran to the net. The two embraced; Michelsen cried. Tiafoe said he had experienced painful five-set losses at the US Open himself. Michelsen said Tiafoe's words moved him.

Primary reporting: [US Open match report](https://www.usopen.org/amp/en_US/news/articles/2026-09-08/frances_tiafoe_makes_epic_comeback_to_defeat_alex_michelsen_at_the_2026_us_open_qfs.html), [US Open interviews](https://www.usopen.org/amp/en_US/news/articles/2026-09-08/tiafoe_on_tearful_us_open_hug_with_michelsen_i_know_how_much_it_hurts.html), [Michelsen's post-match account](https://www.usopen.org/amp/en_US/news/articles/2026-09-08/alex_michelsen_puts_tough_2026_us_open_quarterfinal_loss_in_perspective.html).
